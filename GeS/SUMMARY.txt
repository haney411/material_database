Monolayer GeS (Pmn2_1) Wannier model: summary (2026-10-01)
===========================================================

Structure
---------
- Puckered, phosphorene-like monolayer, space group Pmn2_1 (No. 31). QE finds
  4 symmetry operations with no inversion (point group mm2).
- Axes: x = armchair (the polar axis), y = zigzag, z = vacuum (c = 20 A).
- Lattice: a = 4.40 A, b = 3.68 A, the PBE values from Gomes & Carvalho,
  PRB 92, 085406 (2015), Table I. The cell is fixed at these values.
- Atoms were relaxed at fixed cell (PBE, 45/360 Ry). The run was stopped after
  14 BFGS steps at a total force of 8e-4 Ry/bohr (about 0.02 eV/A).
  Resulting bonds: Ge-S 2.42 A between sublayers, 2.48 A in-plane (x2).
  Layer thickness (Ge-Ge in z): 2.55 A.
  Fractional positions (same order in QE and .win):
    Ge  0.0067216305  0.25  0.5638065245
    S   0.1332783695  0.25  0.4461607974
    Ge  0.5067216305  0.75  0.4361934755
    S   0.6332783695  0.75  0.5538392026

DFT setup
---------
- PBE, no SOC. GBRV USPPs from SSSP efficiency (Ge has 3d in valence).
  Cutoffs 45/360 Ry. No dipole correction is needed, because the n-glide
  (z -> -z) rules out an out-of-plane dipole.
- 12x14x1 grid for SCF, NSCF and Wannier (similar spacing along a and b).
  The NSCF uses 40 bands.

Wannier setup
-------------
- 16 WFs: Ge s,p and S s,p on all 4 atoms. guiding_centres = true (2D).
- exclude_bands 1-10 (Ge 3d), which leaves num_bands = 30.
  The Wannier set holds 10 valence and 6 conduction bands.
- Windows (absolute eV, QE scale): dis_froz_max = 3.2, dis_win_max = 8.0.
  VBM = -1.900 eV (path maximum), so the frozen window ends at VBM+5.1,
  just below the bottom of DFT band 17 (3.27 eV).
- Spread: Omega_I = 34.8, Omega_D = 0.64, total 44.6 A^2.
  Each WF stays assigned to its own atom: S-centred WFs are 0.38-0.53 A from
  the S nucleus, Ge-centred WFs 0.56-0.87 A from Ge (shifted toward the bonds
  and the lone pair). Symmetry-related WFs have identical spreads.
- write_tb, write_hr and write_xyz are on. translate_home_cell is OFF: this
  3.1.0 build writes wrong translated centres to the .xyz (the tb.dat is not
  affected). The .xyz centres are raw, and some sit one lattice vector (c)
  below their atom.

Results (path G-X-S-Y-G, compared with PBE)
-------------------------------------------
- Gap: DFT 1.702 eV, Wannier 1.711 eV. The gap is indirect: VBM on G-X
  (about 3/4 of the way to X), CBM on Y-G. Gomes & Carvalho report 1.69 eV
  (PBE, QE).
- Maximum |E_W - E_DFT|: valence 15 meV; conduction 35 meV below VBM+4 eV,
  70 meV below VBM+5 eV, 0.35 eV below VBM+6 eV.
- Plot: GeS_wannier_bands.png

  (* = chosen)
A was chosen for accuracy over a wide range of optical energies, the same
trade-off as for GaAs. If only the band edges matter, D is the alternative
(valence error under 1 meV, gap error 3 meV).

Cost (4 MPI ranks, -nk 4)
-------------------------
SCF 7 min, NSCF (168 k, 40 bands) 51 min, pw2wannier90 6 min,
DFT path bands (161 k) 48 min, wannier90 under 1 min.
