GaAs bulk Wannier model: summary (2026-09-30)
==============================================

Literature check
----------------
- Ponce et al. (PRB 2021, arXiv:2105.04192) used sp3 on both Ga and As
  (8 WFs, 16 with SOC), experimental lattice constant 10.684 bohr, and
  excluded the Ga 3d semicore bands from the Wannierisation. This setup
  follows that.
- The PBE lattice constant is about 1-2% too large, and the gap nearly closes
  there (Materials Project GGA gives 0.18 eV). The experimental lattice is
  used instead, where PBE gives about 0.5 eV.
- The wannier90 tutorial21 GaAs example also uses a = 10.68 bohr.

Setup (GaAs_wannier/)
---------------------
- PBE, no SOC, Ga PAW (with 3d) + As USPP, cutoffs 70/560 Ry,
  10x10x10 grid.
- 24 DFT bands. The 5 Ga-3d bands (about 12.7 eV below the VBM and lower)
  are excluded, leaving 19 bands for 8 WFs.
- Projections: Ga: s;p and As: s;p. Each WF stays centred on its atom.
- write_tb, write_hr and write_xyz are all on.

Results
-------
- DFT gap: 0.534 eV (direct, at Gamma). Wannier gap: 0.5345 eV.
- Valence bands match DFT within 13 meV.

Disentanglement-window tests (worst conduction-band error vs DFT, below VBM+6 eV):

  froz_max                 Total spread   Worst CB error
  VBM+7.2 eV (chosen)      32 A^2         0.09 eV
  VBM+5.3 eV               24 A^2         0.20 eV
  VBM+4.0 eV               22 A^2         0.77 eV

The chosen window gives wider WFs but accurate conduction bands up to about
7 eV above the VBM, which covers the main absorption peaks.
Production values: dis_froz_max = 13.9 eV, dis_win_max = 20.0 eV (absolute).
Plot: GaAs_wannier_bands.png

Things to know for the absorption calculation
---------------------------------------------
- Frequency ceiling: 8 WFs describe only the 4 lowest conduction bands.
  Absorption above about 6-7 eV will be missing transitions into higher bands.
- Gap correction: the PBE gap (0.53 eV) is far below experiment
  (1.52 eV at 0 K). Use postw90 Kubo (berry_task = kubo) with a
  scissors_shift of about 0.99 eV.
- k-mesh: the Gamma conduction valley is very light in PBE, so Kubo needs a
  dense interpolation grid (80^3 or more, or adaptive refinement) to converge
  near the band edge.
- Cost of a redo: the NSCF step took 40 min. This machine has 4 physical
  cores, so mpirun -np 8 fails; use -np 4.
- Leftovers: the window tests are still in test_A to test_D and can be
  deleted.
